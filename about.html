About

Since Fall 2024 I am s PhD student at University of Massachusetts Amherst , working under the supervision of Martina Rovelli . My main focus is on Higher Category Theory, in particular in the last period I work with higher Morita category using the model of n-complicial set.

I obtained both my Bachelor and Master in Mathemathics at University of Bologna, I wrote my master thesis under the supervision of Giovanni Mongardi and Sofia Tirabassi.
